Four additional public-source videos are now acquired locally: **228,926,191 bytes and 22,348 native frames**. Three contain useful candidate solo views from additional apparent performer/source groups. They broaden the [earlier four demonstrations](solo-boxing-data-2026-09.md). These are partial references, not a labeled six-punch corpus or correct-form reference. Ramsay has had **no inference** and remains model-unseen evaluation material. No training or model selection used these new videos; all three whole groups remain evaluation-only.
