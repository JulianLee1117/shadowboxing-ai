# Live feedback, 30 fps and the first useful form cue

Checked 2026-09-27 against current primary sources and this repository. This is a development roadmap, not a validated coaching product or a claim of 30 observed pose frames per second. The [technique-feedback specification](technique-feedback-spec.md) defines criterion semantics; the [project release gates](../project-plan.md#release-gates) remain canonical. Current measurements belong in [local pose](../local-pose.md), not in a research promise.

## Recommendation

**September 27 clarification:** the user's imperfect recordings are not positive form references. Prioritize external skilled demonstrations and criterion-reviewed counterexamples; use personal clips for recognition/domain testing and optional judgments. The [updated data plan](expert-reference-data.md) supersedes any suggestion below that the user must author the coaching standard or record perfect demonstrations.

Make confirmed actions easy to see immediately when they arrive; measure the remaining recognition delay separately. Pursue genuine sustained pose throughput next, while preparing one narrow coach-reviewed form criterion in parallel. The first criterion should be **`non_punching_hand_guard` during isolated high-guard jabs/crosses**. More punch labels, higher frame rate and a plausible 3D skeleton do not independently validate technique judgments.

### 1. A small action log, not a queue of popups

A useful precedent is Street Fighter 6's live input history, described by [PlayStation's official guide](https://www.playstation.com/en-ph/games/street-fighter-6/everything-you-need-to-know-about-street-fighter-6/). Its known controller inputs differ from inferred movements: our labels arrive after evidence accumulates. Borrow the compact history presentation, not an implication that every movement is known instantly or correctly. [QuickPose's official demos](https://quickpose.ai/demo-apps/) similarly combine a camera overlay, repetition count and concise feedback; these demonstrate a UI pattern, not validated boxing critique.

Proposed first implementation, to test at actual boxing distance:

- Keep the skeleton and round timer visible. Use a fixed-height history of four confirmed detections on desktop, three on mobile and two in short landscape view, newest received first; avoid covering the face or arms. These row counts are the initial implementation choice, not a proven optimum.
- Every event gets its own row, including repeated jabs. Show physical-hand color **and** readable text; color means identity, never correct form. Short name first, lead/rear detail secondary.
- Insert an entire delivered batch immediately. A `+2` means two new detections; show both names. Do not serialize a batch behind animation, speech or a minimum popup lifetime.
- Keep event source time and receipt time distinct. Use receipt order explicitly for this detection log, a unique detection sequence number for repeats, and the observed source-peak timestamp as context. A late finalized event can therefore have an earlier peak than the row below it. Call it a new detection, not the last punch performed; compute combinations from event time separately. Keep rows until pushed out, and reset all history on a new round/source.
- Keep a restrained short arrival pulse and stable text. Tracking loss is a separate current-state message and does not erase confirmed history. No scrolling page, blocking toast, large jump or required interaction mid-round. Reduced-motion mode keeps the same information.
- Treat the row count, dwell time and placement as UX hypotheses. Test repeated same-hand strokes, two-hand batches, late delivery and a long empty interval. Ask whether the boxer can identify what was counted without breaking stance. There is no source establishing one universally optimal fighting-game display for shadowboxing.

A provisional movement signal would be a different feature: label it as tentative, keep it out of confirmed totals, and measure revisions/false cues. Do not make the current final label look earlier by backdating its presentation or counting a prediction before the validated decision policy allows it.

**Delivery acceptance proposal:** no artificial notification queue; each new result appears by the next available render, with measured receipt-to-paint p95 below 100 ms under supported load. This is an engineering target, not achieved evidence or the total punch-to-feedback delay. The implemented display now retains individual history rows and a `+N` receipt pulse. Source-peak timestamps remain context, not notification clocks. The newest delivered batch remains visible subject to the bounded display and explicit overflow indication.

### 2. Define what “30 fps” means

| Quantity             | Measure                                                                                            | Why it matters                                                                                                |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Camera observations  | Distinct source presentation timestamps and their interval distribution                            | The camera must supply actual distinct frames. Requested settings are insufficient.                           |
| Pose observations    | Distinct captured frames successfully processed per second; gaps and replacements                  | A 30 fps video can feed only 20 pose results per second. A held 30 Hz model grid does not add observed poses. |
| Display updates      | Latest result arrival to paint, plus age of the pose shown over the video                          | A 60 Hz display can repeatedly paint the same old skeleton.                                                   |
| Recognition response | Independently annotated peak/end to final event; inference, arbitration and UI portions separately | Faster pixels do not remove required temporal evidence or a deliberate decision hold.                         |

The [W3C Media Capture specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/mediacapture-streams/) explicitly defines `frameRate` settings as configured rather than necessarily achieved, including a lighting-related example. `requestVideoFrameCallback` exposes media timestamps and presentation metadata, but runs on the main thread and may arrive late; its timing is not automatically sensor-to-screen latency. Use its source times to pair pixels and results, and name any unmeasured acquisition time. [Browser API documentation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTMLVideoElement/requestVideoFrameCallback)

At 30 fps, consecutive observations are about 33.3 ms apart; at 60 fps, about 16.7 ms. A brief extremum can fall between either set. Two separate improvements are needed:

- **Temporal coverage:** reduce unnecessary copies, encoding/transport overhead and measured model cost; retain one in-flight job and bounded latest-frame backpressure. For a serial pipeline, sustained 30 results/s needs average service time below 33.3 ms with headroom for variation. Reducing person-detector frequency or using a smaller pose model changes evidence and needs a separate accuracy/person-safety comparison.
- **Image evidence:** exposure time, lighting, focus, framing and occlusion determine whether a sampled hand is usable. Raising frame rate does not uncover the far arm. Shorter exposure can reduce motion blur, but needs sufficient light and can increase noise; compare actual pixels and localization, not just fps. Exposure duration and frame duration are separate controls in [Apple's capture API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfoundation/avcapturedevice/exposureduration). Supported frame-rate ranges depend on the active format. [Apple frame-duration documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfoundation/avcapturedevice/activevideominframeduration)

First inventory the actual device/browser capabilities and inspect achieved timestamps. Do not assume this laptop's built-in camera supports 60 fps or manual shutter in this browser. Web exposure controls are capability-dependent; the [W3C Image Capture draft](https://www.w3.org/TR/image-capture/) is an API definition, not proof of implementation on this device. Prefer a simple lighting/framing improvement before adding manual camera controls to the main UI.

**Performance experiment:** record the same supported setup with current and optimized pipelines, preserving a fixed recognition model. Compare paired accuracy, distinct-frame cadence, p50/p95 frame age, long gaps, actual event delay and UI responsiveness. Run the established 20-minute sustained gate after short functional checks. Test true 60 fps only with a source that actually provides it; offline causal downsampling of that recording can isolate sampling-rate effects, while a separate lower-light trial exposes blur effects. Never synthesize intermediate poses and call them observations.

### 3. First form cue: the other hand during an isolated straight

England Boxing's [coaching handbook](https://www.englandboxing.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/EB_Boxing-Coaching-Handbook-Part-1_v8-002.pdf), printed pp. 67 and 133, describes retaining the non-punching hand at guard and recommends observation before limited, specific feedback. This supports a contextual rubric; it does not prescribe our image thresholds or establish that every guard style is wrong when different.

**Eligible context:** a coach-confirmed beginner high-guard drill, isolated jab or cross, known physical hand/stance, visible head and opposite hand throughout delivery. The initial self-selected pose is not automatically a correct reference. Do not assess this criterion during an intentional parry, another punch, alternate guard, occlusion or uncertain hand assignment.

**Measurement prototype:** retain source RGB evidence; annotate the actual hand region and head reference, then measure relative hand position through the delivery interval. A body wrist landmark is only a proxy. Test its localization against visible pixels before any region threshold. Normalize for scale without pretending to estimate anatomical joint angles. If the relevant hand is hidden, clipped, blurred or implausibly estimated, return `unobservable`, not `fail`.

**Candidate future feedback:** “Keep your rear hand in the selected guard while you jab.” Deliver this only after repeated clearly observed evidence and a validated cue policy, preferably between repetitions/sets. A separate descriptive count can report assessed repetitions and coverage. Do not interrupt every punch with a correction or replace the action history with a form score. `guard_recovery` is the next criterion; combinations, incomplete recovery windows and detector end-time semantics make it a separate validation problem.

The existing [coaching packet tool](../../ml/coaching.py) already supplies blank independent review records, source fingerprints, context/view/outcome fields and disagreement summaries. Use it now rather than turning detection scores or `guardReturn` into correctness labels:

```sh
python3 -m ml.coaching prepare annotated-session.json --video original.webm --output NEW-review-packet
python3 -m ml.coaching check NEW-review-packet/packet.json reviewer-a.json
python3 -m ml.coaching compare NEW-review-packet/packet.json reviewer-a.json reviewer-b.json --output agreement.json
```

The packet input needs independently authored action references. Its judgment vocabulary is `pass`, `fail`, `ambiguous`, `unobservable`, `not_applicable`, or `not_reviewed`; whether feedback is warranted remains separate. The user can author action corrections and provisional observations now; their recordings and self-description do not establish correct-form references. Skilled demonstrations, acceptable variations and assessed faults need their own criterion review. Another reviewer and preserved disagreements are later validation recommendations, particularly before generalizing to other people or publishing broad coaching claims. A label used for training is not independent test evidence, even when authored by an experienced coach.

**Personal labeling now:** the **Correct punches** workflow creates `local-coach-review-2` records with user authorship and provisional form observations; existing v1 records remain compatible. Detector proposals supply navigation only; the user first confirms the actual physical-hand action, “Not a punch,” or “Can't tell.” Confirmed straights optionally offer “Guard held,” “Needs work,” “Can't tell,” and “Not this drill/style,” with notes for acceptable variation. Action progress and resume do not require a form judgment. Unanswered actions remain unreviewed. A manual full-video moment selector covers missed detections without pretending the proposed queue is complete. Original video, events and action references remain separate; each review is pinned to source/video fingerprints. This is supervised labeling, not reinforcement learning, and it does not automatically train or activate a model. The new format is not falsely presented as a completed independent `ml.coaching` review.

A local packet-preparation check produced 23 frozen action references, 14 straights and 28 blank criterion slots. All judgments remain blank. The source was an open round, so no isolated high-guard eligibility can be inferred from those action counts. This exercises the tooling, not the rubric's accuracy.

Validate visible hand/head localization, criterion judgment and emitted correction as three different layers. Include acceptable variation, deliberate defense and ordinary arm movements. Split entire recordings and people before extracting clips; reserve a future day for the personal case. Report corrective-claim precision, false cues/minute, missed deviations and coverage by view/hand. Apply the existing release targets only after a reliable rubric; agreement alone is not accuracy or learning benefit. No universal 90-degree elbow rule, power/force estimate, injury assessment or whole-person form grade is justified here.

### 4. Upgrade the evidence only when a diagnosed failure warrants it

| Route                      | Useful next experiment                                                                                                                                            | Deployment and validity limit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Better single-view 2D pose | Keep the current local backend as baseline; compare a challenger on independently marked wrist/elbow pixels, identities and event outcomes.                       | More landmarks or model confidence are not verified visibility. Retain native score semantics and uncertainty.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Short-window RGB plus pose | If the hand is visible in pixels but pose loses its trajectory, compare a fixed RGB/pose candidate offline on the same whole-recording splits.                    | [RGBPoseConv3D's official implementation](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmaction2/blob/main/configs/skeleton/posec3d/rgbpose_conv3d/README.md) provides an inspectable two-stream baseline. Its clip sampling/training recipe is not an M3 real-time causal pipeline. MMAction2 code is [Apache 2.0](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmaction2); separately check weights/data rights. No benchmark transfer to boxing form.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| New dense/3D RGB models    | Use selected hard frames as an offline localization/occlusion comparison before considering conversion.                                                           | [Sapiens2](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sapiens2), released April 2026, has [308-point pose models](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sapiens2/blob/main/docs/POSE.md), starting at 0.4B parameters for pose; its documented path is GPU-oriented and uses a [custom license](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sapiens2/blob/main/LICENSE.md). [SAM 3D Body](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-3d-body) recovers a mesh from an image, with gated checkpoints/custom terms. [Fast SAM 3D Body](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15603) is a March 2026 acceleration preprint; none of these sources establishes our M3 throughput or boxing-angle accuracy. A reconstructed hidden hand remains an estimate. |
| Second synchronized camera | First record an optional second view as independent review evidence for hard occlusions. Only then evaluate a local synchronized/calibrated multi-view prototype. | Two simultaneous views can expose different anatomy; triangulation also requires calibration, temporal alignment and correspondence. [OpenCap's primary study](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011462) provides a concrete synchronized multi-camera example. Its [hosted workflow](https://www.opencap.ai/get-started) computes kinematics in the cloud; it is not a silent local replacement. A second camera also increases setup and compute cost.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

The boxing-specific [Lahkar et al. study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2022.939980/pdf) used three elite boxers, ten synchronized 60 Hz video cameras and a marker-based reference. Joint-angle agreement varied, especially rotations; results do not validate a monocular laptop estimate. Even a better multi-camera reconstruction needs criterion-specific validation before anatomical coaching claims.

## Concrete order of work

1. **Now:** direct confirmed action history, per-event arrival visibility and timing instrumentation; no change to what counts as a detection merely to make the UI feel faster.
2. **Next engineering gate:** isolate the measured capture/transport/inference bottleneck, benchmark one change at a time, and establish actual sustained observed cadence. Keep the simple single-camera workflow.
3. **In parallel:** use external skilled references, assessed counterexamples and the prepared guard-review packets to settle the context/view rubric; user-authored observations remain separately attributed. Build a video-backed observation extractor, initially visible only in review/development.
4. **After criterion validation:** enable one specific, evidence-linked cue in guided practice, with abstention and a cooldown validated for usefulness. Review outcomes before adding recovery or combinations.
5. **If evidence remains insufficient:** select one RGB or additional-view experiment from the diagnosed failure type. More sensors/models are justified by improved assessability and fewer wrong claims, not novelty.
